* Personal task management under stress (with Org mode)

This repository contains presentation for a talk presented at
EmacsConf2026: https://emacsconf.org/2026/talks/tasks/

Ever had tasks piling up at work, then family matters on top, all with
critical deadlines you cannot miss?  Such moments are often the entry
point to exploring GTD, P.A.R.A., pomodoro, time blocking, time
budgeting, and whatnot.  However, fancy personal task management
techniques often sound logical and appealing, but quickly fall apart
in real life.  Worse, they make your life more difficult because they
encourage recording *even more tasks* that will never be completed and
only pile up, diluting what really matters.  The truth is -- you
cannot complete every possible task and explore every possible idea in
your life. Not even in the short term, because emergencies do
happen. To be useful, personal task management should handle failures,
emergencies, and a plain lack of time and energy.  In this talk, I
will introduce the key principles that took me years of task
management to discover; and that I wish I had known from the
beginning.  I will also show the detailed workflows I built for
myself, with Org mode, for Org mode maintenance and for my real life
job.  This is not a package, but ideas, and workflows that can be
personalized as much as anything in Emacs.
